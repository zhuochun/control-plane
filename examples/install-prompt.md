# Install through your agent

Copy this into your agent:

> Install and set up aicp from https://github.com/zhuochun/control-plane.
> Follow its GETTING_STARTED.md, connect yourself through CLI or MCP, and open
> the review portal when ready.

Then describe what you want it to look after:

> Track our October launch and customer onboarding. Watch Slack
> #launch-readiness and customer email from the three pilot accounts using your
> existing tools. Keep source-backed findings in aicp, investigate actionable
> issues within my authority, and delegate bounded research when useful.
> Bring me material decisions with evidence and a recommendation.

Your agent can configure Interests, source Watchers, and owner context from
that request. Agree on the exact source scope and cadence if they are not already
clear. For recurring checks, have the agent configure its available external
scheduler after the first inspection works. [GETTING_STARTED.md](../GETTING_STARTED.md)
is the setup contract for the agent; the [heartbeat](heartbeat-prompt.md) and
[delegation handoff](delegation-handoff.md) describe operation and recovery.
